Retatrutide is a synthetic peptide that acts on three metabolic receptors at once: GLP-1, GIP and glucagon. It is supplied as a white lyophilized powder in a sealed vial. It is by some distance the compound in this catalogue with the strongest clinical evidence.

Background

GLP-1 agonists — the family semaglutide and liraglutide belong to — changed the treatment of obesity and type 2 diabetes over the past decade. Retatrutide belongs to the next generation: rather than acting on a single receptor, it combines three mechanisms. The glucagon component, which is what sets it apart, is associated in studies with greater energy expenditure.

It is still being developed by Eli Lilly. The phase 2 results were striking enough to be published in the New England Journal of Medicine, and the phase 3 programme is under way. It is not yet an approved medicine in any country.

Handling and storage

Retatrutide is supplied lyophilized (freeze-dried). Handling and storage guidance:

  • Sealed vials: keep the lyophilized powder cold and protected from light; for long-term storage, in a freezer. Keep the vial sealed until you use it.
  • Documentation: note the lot number and keep its certificate of analysis.

Quality and testing

Frequently asked questions

Is the milligram figure on the vial a dose?

No. The figure on the label — 10 mg or 20 mg — is the total amount of peptide the vial contains, not a dose. How much corresponds to a single administration is a clinical decision for your health professional.

Is it approved as a medicine?

No. It has completed phase 2 trials with published results and is in phase 3, but no regulator has approved it yet.

How is purity verified?

Every lot is independently tested at a U.S. laboratory by HPLC with UV detection (purity) and mass spectrometry (identity), and comes with a certificate of analysis for that specific lot. Our purity standard is ≥99.2%.

How should it be stored?

Sealed lyophilized vials keep cold and protected from light; for long-term storage, frozen. Keep the vial sealed until you use it.
