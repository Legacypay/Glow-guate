What Does "Research Use Only" Mean?

Research Use Only (RUO) is a regulatory classification defined by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) that designates products intended exclusively for laboratory research and scientific study. RUO products have not been evaluated by the FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality as they relate to clinical use in humans or animals.

Glow Peptides LLC operates strictly within this classification. Every product we sell is:

  • Intended for in vitro (test tube / cell culture) research in controlled laboratory environments
  • Not approved for human or veterinary therapeutic use
  • Not intended for consumption, injection, inhalation, or topical application to humans or animals
  • Not a dietary supplement, drug, or medical device
  • Sold without therapeutic claims of any kind

Our Commitment to RUO Compliance

Glow Peptides LLC maintains the following practices to ensure compliance with RUO standards:

Practice Details
Product Labeling Every product label includes: "For Research Use Only. Not for human or veterinary use."
Website Disclaimers RUO statements appear on product pages, checkout, order confirmations, and all marketing materials.
Shipping Documents Packing slips and invoices include RUO designation.
Customer Verification All buyers must certify research intent and affirm they will not use products for human or veterinary purposes.
No Medical Guidance We do not provide dosing, administration, reconstitution, or clinical guidance.
Marketing Standards All marketing content is reviewed for RUO compliance. No health claims or therapeutic benefit language.
Employee Training All staff are trained on RUO communication standards per our internal SOPs.

What We Do NOT Provide

Glow Peptides LLC will NEVER provide:
  • Dosing instructions or recommendations
  • Reconstitution or preparation protocols for human use
  • Injection, inhalation, or administration guidance
  • Medical, therapeutic, or health-related advice
  • Claims of therapeutic efficacy, cure, treatment, or prevention of any disease or condition
  • Guidance on use in clinical trials or patient care

Buyer Responsibilities

By purchasing from Glow Peptides LLC, you agree to:

  • Use all products solely for legitimate scientific research purposes
  • Comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws regarding the handling and use of research chemicals
  • Not administer products to humans or animals
  • Not make health claims or therapeutic representations about our products
  • Not resell products for non-research purposes
  • Maintain appropriate records of product use as required by applicable regulations

Right to Refuse Sales

Glow Peptides LLC reserves the right to refuse or cancel any order if we reasonably believe the products will be used in a manner inconsistent with their RUO classification. This includes, but is not limited to, situations where a buyer:

  • Requests dosing or administration guidance
  • States intent to use products on patients or for personal consumption
  • Asks to remove RUO labeling
  • Describes personal health goals as the purpose for purchase

Regulatory References

This policy is informed by the following regulatory framework:

  • 21 CFR Part 809.10(c)(2)(i) — FDA labeling requirements for Research Use Only products
  • Section 503A of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) — Compounding exemptions and research use distinctions
  • FDA Guidance Document, September 2013 — "Distribution of In Vitro Diagnostic Products Labeled for Research Use Only"
